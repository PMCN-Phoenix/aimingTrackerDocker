You also have to think about crosshair placement and your time to shoot. ## Q20. Slendro (2023/3/3)：imprecise vs floating crosshair placement

- **发帖人**：Slendro [Beam]
- **日期**：2023/3/3 09:29
- **来源文件**：`Woohoojin2 - 副本 (8).md`（约第 823-825 行）

> I'll use 2 different terms for the subjects: Imprecise crosshair placement, and floating crosshair placement. (You exhibit both). Imprecise crosshair placement is when actively are trying to preaim an angle or corner, and you are not on the EXACT point where the enemy is going to be (think above headshot hight, in the wall, a bit under hs hight, etc). This makes for bad crosshair placement, as it requires you to have better microadjusting capabilites, and in general makes your shots harder to hit. Floating crosshair placement is when you are looking at nothing, i.e., your crosshair is in transition. This is often not really a problem, as it often is a short duration, but when you peek out with a floating crosshair (looking at nothing, as seen in 0:57), you are at a massive disadvantage.
