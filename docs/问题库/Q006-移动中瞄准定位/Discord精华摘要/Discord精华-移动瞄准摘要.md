> 对应问题：新手"先停→再瞄→再开枪"节奏，如何过渡到"减速过程中完成瞄准、急停瞬间准星已在头上"

## 一、核心结论（12 条，每条带原文支撑）

### C1. 正确顺序是"边减速边瞄准"，不是"停稳再瞄"
急停减速只需要约 80ms，远短于瞄准所需时间，所以必须把瞄准任务塞进减速窗口里完成。
> "We don't need to stop and then aim. We should try to aim while slowing down or pre aim right or left of the head and make sure that the movement while slowing down will move the crosshair onto our enemies head... It only takes 80ms to get from full movement speed with a vandal to being slow enough to be accurate. That is very fast. Way faster then it takes us to aim which means we can spend more of the time moving aiming and then stop at the time where we have gotten close enough to their head and all we need to do is micro-adjust which we can do while slowing down and fire when it is all been done."
> —— **Dannew (2024/2/7)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (4).md（热图分析，详见专节）

### C2. 瞄应该在"停"之前完成，停完只剩"扣扳机"
> "Practice aiming while moving (Aiming should be done before stopping your movement so all you have to do is shoot)"
> —— **Mang Tomas (2024/3/25)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (4).md

### C3. "移动只是瞄准的机会，不是等待瞄准的时间"
边打边跑（deadzoning drill式打法）不调整准星等于白给：
> "On some fights you fight it like youre doing a deadzoning drill and don't actually adjust your crosshairs to get onto the opponents head while you are moving. Remember the movement is just an opportunity to aim"
> —— **Sophia [Aims] (2023/11/16)**，Woohoojin1.md / Woohoojin2.md（精华提取文件 §残局1v1 收录）

### C4. 对枪中的 strafe 本质是"用 tracking 把准星挂在敌人头上"
> "strafing is a game of chicken almost, the one who stops first will be less accurate... strafing is mostly tracking in order to keep your crosshair on the enemys head... Good crosshair placement brings their heads closer to your crosshair, good movement and peeking make you harder to hit and can make you peek instantly on their heads"
> —— **vlad? (2025/4/9)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (2).md

### C5. 用身体（移动）代替鼠标微调——"microadjust instead of using my movement"是典型错误
> "I was pretty confident I had it in the bag until I went over the second video 'Everything you need to master my overaim drill' and saw I was making a bunch of small mistakes like microadjusting instead of using my movement etc."
> —— **vlad? (2025/4/9)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (2).md
> （即：差一点点没对齐时，应该挪步子/微调移动方向把准星"走"过去，而不是纯用手小幅挪鼠标。）

### C6. 正确的开火节奏：先大 flick 落到附近 → 再小修正 → 最后才扣扳机
> "Everytime you peek an enemie/one peeks you, where you need to make a bigger flick to land on their head, you flick and imideatly shoot (and mostly whiff). It is better make the first flick, and then micro adjust onto your target with a second smaller flick. Make sure you really think that you are on their head when you are shooting"
> —— **Your Internet (2025/3/8)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (2).md

### C7. "shoot before ready"（没停稳/准星没到位就开枪）是最常见的第一枪失准原因
> "You tend to shoot well before you are ready. Meaning, either your crosshair is not on their head yet, or you have not stopped moving yet. This is causing your first bullet accuracy to suffer."
> —— **Holmster [AIM] (2024/3/5)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (4).md
> "7:45 - you shoot before you've stopped moving, you can see it on the movement inaccuracy graph and your shot goes to his legs... 9:17 - pog reaction time/prefire, you start moving at the second burst though so it's inaccurate"
> —— **notarock (2023/7/7)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (5).md

### C8. 追击移动目标时要"瞄到目标前方"（aim past moving targets），且动作要更平滑
> "Aim technique is costing your fights that you really should be converting; this game was a 13/9 off of those merits alone. **Aim past moving targets to contain them. Aim more smoothly when having to take wider shots**"
> —— Woohoojin 给 Valps 的笔记（被 Valps 本人引用回帖），**Valps (90/90)LZ (2024/2/23)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (4).md
> 另一条佐证（横向移动量大的目标难打）："There's A LOT of horizontal movement from him, so naturally it's gonna be a hard shot... tracking Brim's pathing would at least be less horizontally challenging" —— Woohoojin2 - 副本 (2).md

### C9. DM 里练法：边 strafe 边把准星挂对方头上盯 2 秒，等他停稳再开枪
> "try to be more patient and actually shoot when you are on their heads, you almost always miss your first bullet (Guardian only DM helps a lot in my opinion, you can also just track their head for 2sec while straffing and then try to shoot when they are stopped)"
> —— **Scaff [PKR] (2023/3/3)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (8).md

### C10. 清角/peek 时准星要"贴墙跟着墙角走"，而不是预瞄一个死点
> "at 1:44, you peek out from flowers pre-aiming backsite and ignoring secret. instead of pre-aiming, your crosshair has to track the wall as you swing out so you're more prepared for anyone peeking you instead of your crosshair swinging past them"
> —— **Zabil [GUP] (2023/8/6)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (5).md
> "Practice rounding or tracking the outside of corners and only pre-aim if the corner you are about to swing is a long straight angle"
> —— **Mang Tomas (2024/3/25)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (4).md

### C11. Pre-aim 还是 Tracking，取决于角度优劣（frame advantage）
> "Pre-aim is placing your crosshair in the wall so it'll be in the correct place when you swing. Tracking is like locking your crosshair to an angle to clear it. Generally: Pre-aim when you have angle disadvantage (angle you're swinging is closer to you than the enemy). Pre-aim when you have info on enemy position. Tracking when you have angle advantage (you are further away from the angle than the enemy)"
> —— **frenzy (2025/1/21)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (2).md

### C12. 清角要平滑（strafetracking），不要锯齿式（jagged/staccato）急拉
> "When you clear it, it's like you're tryin to slice the pie but your crosshair moves up or down and your movements aren't a smooth clear, so it's very jagged... Try strafetracking — it sounds counterintuitive, but strafetracking helps your crosshair maintain a smooth movement while following a target, which valyn has mentioned before to be very useful when clearing angles when peeking, leading to easier duels since you know your crosshair is already adjusted."
> —— **mayafuni [BTR!] (2025/3/8)**，Woohoojin2 - 副本 (2).md

## 三、Dannew 热图分析专节（本数据集中最核心的一段）

**人物**：Dannew ｜ **时间**：2024/2/7 21:46 ｜ **出处**：Woohoojin2 - 副本 (4).md（精华提取文件 §对枪技巧 [3] 同文收录）

### 他做了什么
他在 DM（deathmatch）复盘里，给一位"停稳→再瞄准"型玩家做了一张热图：标注出玩家"减速到可以精确射击的那一帧"时，敌人头部（绿点）相对于准星的位置。只统计每次交火的第一个急停。然后拿自己 DM 的热图做对照，再把两张热图合并、去掉最极端样本后，画出"双方重新调整所需的范围"。

### 他发现了什么（数据结论）
- **被复盘者的问题**：准星在"已经停稳的那一刻"离敌人头很远，意味着接下来要么被迫大 flick、要么站着不动原地瞄。
- **Dannew 自己的做法**：停的时候准星离头也不算特别近，但他是"边减速边靠近头"，所以停完只剩 micro-adjust。
- **量化结论**：去掉极端样本后，"较容易的那 50% 调整量"区间显示——**Dannew 有 50% 的时候需要做的二次调整，比对方短得多**。
- **80ms 定律**：Vandal 从全速到可精确射击只要 80ms，远短于一次瞄准，所以"边动边瞄"在物理时间上完全可行，且能让自己在对方屏幕上"还没站定"时就打出那颗子弹。

### 他给的方法论（对新手直接可执行）
1. 拉出去时先一个大 flick 落到角度/交火区域，目标是"落点尽量靠近敌人头"。
2. Pre-aim 在头的左/右侧一点，**让减速过程中的横向滑动自然把准星带过头**。
3. 进入减速后只做短距离 micro-adjust，全程可在减速中完成。
4. 减速完成的瞬间开火——此时在对方屏幕上你还没完全站定。
5. 愿意为"准星离头更近"多滑几 ms：你变成更难打的目标，省下的瞄准时间差不多。
6. 热图链接（puu.sh 截图）与他自己 DM 的对照录像（youtu.be/UK43hBlwlFs）都附在原消息里。

### 他自己承认的局限
- 样本量不大（漏掉了"来不及减速就死"的交火）。
- 他自己也做不到每次都贴头停，他只是"努力这么做"。
- DM 数据、不同地图，仅供参考。

## 五、争议点 / 反面意见 / 边界条件

1. **"边移边瞄"不是让你边移边开枪。** 多份复盘同时强调：移动中开枪 = movement inaccuracy（蓝条），子弹飘到腿上（notarock 7:45；副本(5).md:604 "you STRAFE away from the fight and movement inaccuracy hits nothing"）。"边移边瞄"的终点仍然是急停后那颗精确首发。
2. **"移动" vs "瞄准"是二选一的折中，不是无限叠加。** Slendro (2024/6/21)："The biggest problem that you seem to have is your accuracy, which comes from your terrible aim when moving. Either you move less, or you try to aim whilst moving more." —— 即两条路都可以，前提是别半吊子。
3. **"急停站定补枪"和"边移边甩"在不同场景相反。** kyle (2024/4/5) 批评：打完一个敌人立刻开始移动而不是站定收第二个头，是坏习惯。Dannew 方案适用于"对枪展开的那个瞬间"，多杀转移时该站定就站定。
4. **Jett 刀是例外：本来就能 moving accuracy。** Kiraie (2024/12/16)："one big strength of jett knives is the ability to be fully accurate while moving, why not jump around the corner instead of running" —— 近战武器规则不同，不能类推到 Vandal。
5. **手抖（mouse shake）不是人人都要管。** Slendro (2023/3/28) 明确说："this point doesnt really matter unless you have problems with tracking the enemy or hitting clean shots."
6. **"strafing spam"本身被职业选手观察为未必好。** Mang Tomas (2024/3/25) 建议学员观察职业选手："do they A D strafe spam or not, counterstrafe or not, hold W when peeking or not" —— 说明 AD spam 不是政治正确，要 copy 的是 f0rsaken 那种"反方向 + 不定时蹲"的不可预测性。
7. **50/50 dry peek 永远输一半，练移动瞄准救不了决策。** 副本(3).md:1031 的长回复："when you swing a dry 1v1, you WILL lose about half of the time... practice taking ADVANTAGED fights that aren't 50/50s. Swing off a teammate. Jiggle peek and then swing wide."
