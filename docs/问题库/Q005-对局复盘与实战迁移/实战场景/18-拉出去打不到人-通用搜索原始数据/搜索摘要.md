# 18-拉出去打不到人（swing out and can't hit）通用搜索摘要

> 主题：VALORANT 拉出/peek 时瞄准落空——预瞄、急停、peek 类型、peeker's advantage、flick 过头
> 搜索时间：2026-10-14
> 搜索方式：general_search（英/中）+ web_fetch 精读 2 篇
> 结论一句话：打不到人的主因不是"反应慢"，而是**准星没提前在头线（peek 后才开始找目标）+ 没停稳就开枪（moving inaccuracy）+ flick 完立刻扣扳机没有二次微调窗口**。

## 一、英文教学站（已精读全文，★=精读页）

### ★1. BoostRoom — Peeking Like a Pro: Jiggle, Shoulder, and Wide Swings Explained
URL: https://boostroom.com/blog/peeking-like-a-pro-jiggle-shoulder-and-wide-swings-explained
发布：2026-04-14

关键原文：
- "A 'good peek' is half movement and half crosshair placement. Your crosshair should already be at head height and aimed where the enemy is likely to be. **If you peek and then try to aim, you're late. If you aim first and then peek, you're ready.**"
- 停-射节奏："move (to be hard to hit) → stop briefly (to become accurate) → burst (2–4 bullets) → move again. **If you shoot while moving at medium or long range, you often lose to someone who simply stopped for their first burst.**"
- Wide swing 技术链："Pre-aim: place your crosshair at head height where the defender will be. → Swing out wide. → Stop: become accurate for a brief moment. → Burst: controlled shots. → Reset: if you miss, strafe back to cover or reposition—**don't freeze**."
- 最常见 peek 死因清单（直接对应"拉出去打不到/被秒"）："Dry wide swinging common angles with no utility and no trade / Peeking multiple angles at once / **Shooting while moving at medium/long range** / Repeeking instantly after being spotted / **Standing still in the open after missing a burst** / Jiggle peeking predictably so you get prefired."
- 反 Operator 模式：shoulder peek 骗枪 → 队友趁换弹 timing 一起 swing。

### ★2. ProSettings.net — How To Peek In VALORANT and Clear Angles Like A Pro
URL: https://prosettings.net/blog/how-to-peek-in-valorant/
发布：2025-08-12

关键原文：
- "When you swing from an angle, **you should only be using your left and right keys, and your crosshair should not be moving.** You have to pre-aim the crosshair in such a way that when you swing the crosshair ends up on the enemy's head or close to it, so you can micro-adjust. ... Try to imagine that there is no wall in front of you and you have to strafe to hit the enemy."
- Peek 类型定义：Normal / Jump / Wide / Jiggle / Crouch（"Crouch peek: usually not recommended, but a one-time trick to confuse the enemy"）。
- Peeker's advantage 解释："You are holding an angle, the enemy peeks and kills you while they appear to be moving. On the **peeker's screen, they stopped and shot**, but there's a delay between server and your client — that's why it looks like they shot while moving."
- 练习法：DM 里别管输赢，主动练不同 swing 距离；靶场 100 bots 躲墙后 swing-shoot-headshot。

## 二、Peeker's Advantage（官方机制解释）

### 7. Riot Games — Peeking into VALORANT's Netcode
URL: https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/peeking-valorants-netcode
发布：2020-07-28
- 官方定量结论："This means that **peekers have ~141ms** [of reaction advantage over the holder, in the modeled case]."
- 公式本质：holder 要活下来，反应时间必须 < peeker 反应时间 − 双方网络延迟 − 网络缓冲。

### 8. VALORANT 官方 — 04: On Peeker's Advantage & Ranked
URL: https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/04-on-peeker-s-advantage-ranked/
发布：2020-05-13
- "It's <ENEMY'S CLIENT FRAMERATE> + <ENEMY'S 1-WAY NETWORK LAG> + <SERVER FRAMERATE> + <YOUR 1-WAY NETWORK LAG> + <NETWORK INTERP DELAY>。NETWORK INTERP DELAY = 7.8125ms"
- 官方中文站（意大利语版转引）："Il tempo di reazione medio di un essere umano è di 247 ms. Il vantaggio dell'attaccante in VALORANT è **in media di 40-70 ms**."

### 9. Dot Esports — Peeker's advantage in VALORANT explained
URL: https://dotesports.com/valorant/news/peekers-advantage-in-valorant-explained
- 定义复述："the player peeking around a corner has an edge over the player holding the angle in networked gameplay."

**对本主题的意义**：peeker's advantage 是"拉出去的人先天多 40–141ms"。所以"我拉出去打不到、架枪的反而先秒我"不是你反应差——如果你先看到人却打空，问题在**你的准星/停枪**，不在网络优势。

## 三、急停 / 移动中开枪 / flick 过头

### 10. DCPROSENS — How to Improve Aim in Valorant: The Complete 2026 Guide
URL: https://dcprosens.com/blog/how-to-improve-aim-valorant-2026
- "In Valorant, **you cannot shoot accurately while moving.** Counter-strafing = press the opposite direction key momentarily to stop momentum before shooting. Moving right → tap A → first bullet accurate → fire. **The window for 'fully stopped' after counter-strafing is approximately 60–100ms.**"

### 11. Switchblade Gaming — Valorant Aim Training: 15-Minute Range Routine
URL: https://www.switchbladegaming.com/valorant/aim-training-guide-2/
- "The moment your movement fully halts is called the **plant** — Valorant's first-bullet accuracy activates approximately **2 frames after a full plant**. Counter-strafing gets you there in 1–2 [taps]."

### 12. GearUP — How to Develop Your Aim in Valorant
URL: https://www.gearupbooster.com/blog/valorant-aim-guide-2025.html
- "You move to a peek, you stop, and only then you fire. **Skipping this step is the most common reason new players miss easy shots.** Pair with micro-adjustments: instead of huge flicks, place your crosshair near the target and tap to refine."

### 13. AimTrainerPro — Best Aim Training for Valorant Players
URL: https://aimtrainerpro.online/blog/best-aim-training-for-valorant
- "The key with flicks is to **slow down enough to be accurate first**, then let speed build. **An overshot flick that needs a second correction is slower overall than a controlled snap that lands on the first try.**"
- "Valorant tracking is short and sharp, never a long sweeping arc."

### 14. mrhedly — Top 5 Tips To Increase Your Aim Accuracy In Valorant
URL: https://mrhedly.com/top-5-tips-to-increase-your-aim-accuracy-in-valorant/
- Counter-strafe："if moving left using 'A', immediately tap 'D' before shooting. Fire only when your character has come to a complete stop."
- "Keep your focus locked on your crosshair and allow it to 'place' itself naturally over the enemy. **Click only when your crosshair and the enemy perfectly overlap.**"

### 15. ValoLab（土耳其语，关键句已摘录）
URL: https://valolab.net/valorant-aim-gelistirme.html
- Flick 两段论："Flick = fast approach + controlled stop on target. **Speeding up only the first part produces overshoot.** Ask yourself how many pixels your crosshair overshoots the target each time."

## 五、综合归因："拉出去打不到人"的 5 个技术原因（按出现频率排序）

| # | 原因 | 证据来源 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **准星没预瞄在头线**，peek 出去才开始找敌人（"peek then aim = late"） | BoostRoom、ProSettings、MetaBot、NGA |
| 2 | **没停稳就开枪**，moving inaccuracy 把子弹打到腿上；急停后 60–100ms（约 2 帧）才恢复首发精确 | DCPROSENS、Switchblade、GearUP、mrhedly |
| 3 | **flick 完立刻扣扳机**，没有"大 flick 落点 → 二次微调 → 确认 → 开枪"的窗口；overshoot 后补正反而更慢 | AimTrainerPro、ValoLab、NGA |
| 4 | **Dry peek / 无信息无补枪单人拉角**，且同时暴露在多个角度 | BoostRoom、ProSettings |
| 5 | 拉出去后 miss 一枪就**僵在原地（freeze/stand still in open）**，不回掩体也不重新定位 | BoostRoom、Q007 Discord 挖掘（Rone "freeze up after peeking"） |

> 注：peeker's advantage（40–141ms）是拉枪方的**先天优势**，所以"打不到"通常不是网络/反应问题，而是上述 1–3 的瞄准执行问题。
