# Q004.5 英文社区原始数据（2026-10-17 抓取）

## 1. fpstrain.us — Click Timing Drill（核心）

- **关键警告**："Score matters, but it can rise because you learned one spawn pattern or took more risks. Better markers include stable accuracy across three runs, lower tension, fewer repeated miss types, and better transfer in game footage."（分数上涨可能只是学会了某一种出生点模式）
- **动作序列**："Move your eyes to the target first, then let the hand follow the visual decision. Decelerate into the target instead of dragging through it."
- **常见错误**：clicking as soon as the target appears / tracking too long before taking a clean window / mistaking reaction time for timing quality
- **搭配建议**："For tactical shooters, combine it with crosshair placement or static clicking."
- **进阶标记**：misses cluster less around target edges；能说出每个 miss 的原因（overshoot/undershoot/late read/early click/tension）

## 2. fpstrain.us — How to Actually Improve Your Reflexes（核心）

- **反应的两层结构**：Raw reaction time ≈ 固定；Functional reflex（anticipation + placement + audio + motor patterns）= 可训练大头
- **Crosshair placement 是最大免费收益**："That collapses the task from detect→decide→move→click down to confirm→click, which can feel 150–300 ms faster than a true reaction."
- **Eye discipline**："keep your gaze on likely enemy positions (not on your own crosshair)… Pre-fixating the spot an enemy will appear shaves your perception time because the target lands on your fovea instead of your periphery."

## 3. playerbenchmark.com — How Pro Players Train Their Aim

- **Multiclick 120 的归类**：放在 **Target Switching** 类（与 Bounce 180 TS 并列）——"train the ability to efficiently redirect your crosshair between distinct targets, combining elements of flicking and tracking"（不是静态点击！）
- 职业队伍 8-12h/天中 aim training 只占 30-60 分钟；"Crosshair placement eliminates the need for dramatic flicks. Positioning determines whether you even need to aim at all."
- 2020 meta-analysis（Psychological Bulletin）：deliberate practice 解释感知运动任务约 26% 表现方差

## 4. aimtrainerspro.online — Improve Aim Guide

- "Reset instantly after every engagement so you're never caught aiming low walking into the next one."（每次交火后立刻复位——实战迁移的关键纪律）
- "Anticipate common enemy positions per map rather than reacting to motion"

## 5. aimtrainerspro.com — Peek Practice（pre-aim vs reaction 数学）

- "La ventana de peek es más corta que una reacción humana en frío"（peek 窗口短于冷启动反应）→ pre-aim 把"反应问题"转成"确认-点击问题"
- 常见错误：准星留在掩体间的死区、见人扫射、瞄身体而不是头线

## 6. kordu.tools — Aim Improvement（学术引用）

- **Palomaki et al. 2019, Frontiers in Psychology**：aim trainer 训练收益取决于训练情境与实战情境的相似度；抽象点击迁移差，game-specific 情境迁移好

## 7. 学术检索（WebSearch 定点回源）

### Georgopoulos et al.（UMN，猕猴 2D 瞄准任务）
- 练习使轨迹空间变异性指数级下降，但 **RT 几乎不变**；峰值速度略升
- **空间不确定性增加轨迹变异性；时间不确定性无影响**
- 目标中途变更：手部动作指令是连续实时过程，可被新目标随时打断；眼睛则总是先完整 saccade 到第一/第二目标
- → 结论：预判改变的是**运动质量**不是**启动时间**；预测失败时轨迹变异放大

### Kieras, ONR 2005（EPIC 认知架构综述）
- 指向类动作 S-R 兼容性极高：目标一经识别即可快速启动运动，**无 Hick 定律效应、无运动编程延迟**（Wright 2004: 2/6 备选 RT 254 vs 257ms；Kveraga 2002/2005 saccade 同样）
- → Multiclick 里预瞄省的不是"反应时间"（本来就省不出多少），而是**注意力定位 + 大位移**成本

### Adam 等人（Europe PMC 10089763）
- 外周线索预提示目标位置时，**RT 随线索-目标距离增加而上升**——预瞄收益来自注意力先行定位，且对目标引导的手部反应距离效应更强
- 眼动与手部反应有独立神经表征，但共享超模态注意空间

### Journal of Neurophysiology 2018（saccade 历史偏差）
- 预期目标位置产生**历史偏差**：预期对→更快；预期错→需解决"预期 vs 实际"冲突，SRT 延迟
- "Increased preparation time reduces, but does not abolish, action history bias"——预期偏差靠加长准备时间也只能减弱不能消除
- → 预判是双刃剑：正确预测加速，错误预测附加冲突代价（"预期陷阱"的机制基础）
- 引用 Rosenbaum 1980（运动准备必须完成才能启动）vs Haith 2016（启动与准备过程独立）之争
